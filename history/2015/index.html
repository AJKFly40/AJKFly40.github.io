Matt Thoman took the 2015 title. The league was still being run on a spreadsheet: what remains of the season is the commissioner's own table of final records, and 32 of the 85 games his table counts survive.

What survives of 2015

The league did not move to Sleeper until 2018, so there is no game-by-game record of 2015. Two different things survive, and they are not equally complete.

The commissioner's own table of final records is complete — he wrote it down at the time, and it is what the standings below are. It is a stated fact, not a sum over surviving games, which is why it can name every one of the 12 teams with a real record.

The game log is not. 32 games from 2015 survive in it, against the 85 games that table counts, and they are not a random sample: the commissioner logged the games he judged notable at the time — close finishes, big scores — and left the rest. So the section below is a list of surviving games, not a week-by-week account of the season, and no season total, average or streak anywhere on this site is built from it. Nothing at all survives from weeks 10 and 11.

Playoff bracket

No bracket survives from 2015

The archive recovered games, not playoff structure. Who played whom in the 2015 playoffs, and in what order, is not recorded anywhere the league still has. The title itself is not in doubt — Matt Thoman is named as champion in the commissioner's own record — but the path to it is gone. Any playoff games that do survive are in the list below, marked as playoff weeks.

The draft

No draft record survives from 2015

Drafts were run in a room, on paper, and the spreadsheet the rest of 2015 comes from never held one. The league's draft history on this site starts in 2018, when Sleeper began recording it. Nothing is reconstructed here and nothing is guessed.
