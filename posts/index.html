The newsroom

The feed

Short items, one fact each, most recent first. This is what the desks file on the days that do not carry an article — a desk noticing something rather than an article that ran out of room. Each one names the sheet it came from.

The long-form archive

Nothing on the wire yet

No post has been filed. Through the offseason the paper is deliberately quiet, at roughly one archival item a month; from the draft onward the feed carries something most days, and the wire fires whenever a transaction lands.

Every item that appears here will name the fact sheet it was written from. That is not a footnote on this publication — it is the whole of what it claims. A desk has no memory of this league; it is handed a sheet of facts queried out of the record, and a number that is not on the sheet cannot reach the page.

The desks

Bylines, not people

Six beats, four of them staffed. A desk covers its beat for as long as the league lasts, which is the reason there are desks here and not invented writers.

The Beat Desk@WKPGameday

Recaps, previews, straight coverage. Anchors the site.

The Records Desk@WKPVault

Every result against the full span of the league's record.

The Numbers Desk@WKPNumbers

Luck ratings, expected wins, bench points. Curious, not cruel.

The Wire@WKPWire

FAAB, claims, trades. Fires when something lands.

The Ledger@WKPLedger

Prize money. Who is up, who is chasing the last ten dollars.

Not staffed. The data this desk would need — the payout history, the trophy's custody log — is not on record, and a desk inventing it would be worse than a desk that stays quiet.

Trophy Watch@WKPTrophy

Where the trophy is, who has it, how long.
