How to read these records — two scopes on every figure

Era. Sleeper has run this league since 2018, so everything from that season forward is a complete, game-by-game log. 2014–2017 survive only as the commissioner's own notes — a curated list of notable games, not a log of every matchup played. A real record-setting score from those years is exactly the kind of thing he would have written down, so single-week highs and lows below still reach back to 2014, with every figure drawn from that stretch marked Partial season. Everything that depends on a complete log — season and career totals, streaks, and win–loss results — is built only from 2018 onward and marked Since 2018 instead, so nothing below reads as an all-time claim it can't back up.

Phase. The single-week highs and lows count every game the league has played, playoffs included — the highest week in league history is a playoff game, and so is the lowest — and are marked Playoffs included. Season totals, career totals and streaks stop when the regular season does, and are marked Regular season. The two chips sit side by side on every headline figure and every table below, because a scope stated on one axis and left silent on the other is how two different things come to look the same.

A figure marked Partial season is a floor, not a settled fact.
