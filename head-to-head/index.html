What a Partial cell means, and what "No record" does not mean

Hank Newman, Jason Kent and Ross played 2014–2016 but have no head-to-head block of their own in the commissioner's spreadsheet — they only ever appear inside an aggregate "Defunct Franchises" bucket, which records 64 games in total. 25 of those 64 survive in the game log with both sides named, and appear as real pairings below; the other 39 name no opponent at all, so they count toward a manager's own record but appear in no cell of this grid.

A cell marked Partial may be missing games that were actually played — read it as a lower bound, not a settled rivalry. And a cell reading “No record” is not a claim that the two never met: it means they were in the league together and the log kept no game between them, which is true of 17 pairings here. A dash means the opposite — that the data supports the negative outright.
