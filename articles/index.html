The newsroom

Articles

Long-form pieces, filed by desk. Every one of them was written from a fact sheet queried out of the league's own record, and every one carries that sheet at the foot of the page — the facts it used, the ones it had to qualify, and the things the record does not have.

The short-form feed

Nothing filed yet

No article has been published. The paper is deliberately quiet through the offseason — roughly one archival post a month — and the desks below start filing against the season's own calendar: a full recap and the stat dumps on Tuesday, the waiver story on Wednesday, previews on Thursday, and the big Sunday recap late that night.

When a piece lands it appears here the same hour. Publishing is automatic; nothing waits on the commissioner, and nothing reaches this page without every number in it having been traced back to a query first.

The desks

Bylines, not people

The paper is written by desks rather than by invented writers. A desk covers a beat for as long as the league lasts; a character would have to be kept in voice for twelve seasons and would curdle into a bit.
