How a keeper is priced

Rule one

Up to 3 keepers

No roster may keep more than 3 players, however cheap the rest look.

Rule two

The price is a round

Keeping a player costs the round before the one he was drafted in: a fifth-rounder costs your fourth. Round 1 is the floor, so a first- and a second-rounder both cost a first. The price follows the player, not the manager — a player picked up since the 2025 draft keeps the round whoever drafted him set.

Rule three

A keeper needs a pick you own

A keeper has to sit in a round you actually own a pick in. If that pick is traded away, he moves up to the nearest earlier round you do own — marked Moved up below, with the reason. If more players are priced in a round than you hold picks in it, only as many as you hold can sit there; the commissioner settles which of them moves, and the prices below are each player on his own.
